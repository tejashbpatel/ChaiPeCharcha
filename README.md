
# ☕ Chai Pe Charcha — Daily Talk

**Ek chai, ek sawaal, poori charcha.**
A warm, chai-stall-styled iOS app for India where families, neighbours and friends gather around one daily question.

> Status: 🧪 TestFlight beta (iOS)

---

## What is it?

Chai Pe Charcha brings back the feeling of sitting together over a cup of chai and talking about the day. Instead of endless feeds, you get one thoughtful question a day and the people who matter to you.

## Features

- **Daily Question** — one debate-worthy question every day. Share your take and see what others think.
- **Parivar (family circles)** — a private space for your family, with a festival and puja event area: date, RSVPs and who's bringing what.
- **Mohalla (neighbourhood)** — invite-only chat for one society or building (around 100 people). Created by one person, joined through an iCloud invite link, with report and block built in.
- **Chai Break** — get matched for a short chat with someone new.
- **Share cards** — share your take as a branded Chai-logo card.
> Some features are still being built. The beta starts with the Daily Question and Parivar circles.

## Design

A warm chai-stall look: cream, saffron and deep brown.

## Tech

- Swift and SwiftUI
- CloudKit (iCloud) for sync and invites
- Xcode, iOS
