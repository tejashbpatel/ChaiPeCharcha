Community rules

These apply to everything you write in a parivar or a mohalla, including posts, replies, event chat and names.

  • Be kind. No abuse, hate, harassment, threats or bullying.
  • No foul language. This includes slurs, insults and sexual or violent content.
  • Respect privacy. Don't share other people's names, addresses, phone numbers, photos or messages outside the group. Don't post private information about anyone without their consent.
  • Keep invite codes private. Give a code only to a person you know. A code works once and expires after 7 days.
  • No spam, scams or illegal content, and no pretending to be someone else.

Reporting, blocking and removal

  • Anyone can report a post, reply or chat message from its menu. Reported items go to the person who created the group, who can remove them.
  • Anyone can block a person. You then no longer see their posts, replies or messages.
  • The person who created a group can remove a neighbor from a mohalla. A removed person's posts and replies are deleted, and they can't rejoin.
  • You can leave a group at any time, and the person who created a group can delete it.
    • When someone reports a message, post or profile, a copy (the reporter’s name, the group name and a short excerpt) is sent to the app owner for moderation.
